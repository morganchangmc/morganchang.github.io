A carbon fibre tail fairing designed to reduce the drag of the rider and bike. I modelled it in SolidWorks, checked it with FEA and CFD in Ansys, and then helped build it.

## Wet layup
{% include img.html src="fairing-layup.jpg" caption="We used a wet layup method, cured under vacuum. The untrimmed part is shown after curing." %}
Careful layup work cut bridging and dry spots by about 20% compared with the previous iteration.
